Vman - VFX/AI Technical Director & Polymath Enthusiast

Like Larry from Gumball, I wear a different uniform every time you see me. I started in fine arts, but physical canvas took too long to dry. Once I switched to digital, I didn't have to wait for paint to dry, but everything felt way too still. Once things were moving, they lacked smoke and skulls on fire, so I started connecting nodes and coding VEX. But that part lacked any real meaning, so I decided to write some different lines to give it a motive. And... when I asked the machine for help, it answered: 'I'm afraid I can't do that, Dave'—leaving me with no logical choice but to spend the next few few few moments later proving to the AI that yes, it absolutely could do that. Pure efficiency of standard procedure... but you know that.

Efficiency is rarely standard procedure. I remember when my smart washing machine refused to sync with my phone, I completely bypassed the initial ticket. Somehow I managed to get that thing to do something, but then I heard it play Schubert and immediately thought: wouldn't it be way more incredible if it played the Silent Hill theme instead? It would be the most amazing thing ever—every single time I go fetch my clean socks, I’d tremble in fear. Naturally, the machine still doesn't connect to my phone, but that fear is the exact motivation to continue writing my horror feature film. (Which, for the record, has absolutely nothing to do with dirty laundry, but writing a movie isn't exactly the kind of task you just put on hold).

Since we don't have a DeLorean to bend time and fix the hours we just wasted on brilliant nonsense, we have to hurry up. After all, we create not because we need, but because we fucking can. And I don't want all those ideas to be lost in time, like tears in rain. Time to bye.
